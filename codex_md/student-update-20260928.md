# Student calendar, curriculum and authentication update — 2026-09-28

University timetable import now marks its events for weekly/daily display and hides them in the monthly calendar, including the monthly Android feed. Existing imported event IDs are backfilled when the additive column is introduced. Ordinary events retain their existing visibility. Semester defaults are March 1–June 30 and September 1–December 31; individual start/end dates remain editable and persist with each user's term draft. Imports still preview national-holiday exclusions and prevent duplicate occurrences.

Course planning has separate current-major and exploration views. Up to eight department/year/track selections persist per authenticated user, with revision checks preventing one tab from overwriting another. Cards compare major and graduation credit requirements; they do not sum overlapping credit recognition or claim graduation eligibility. Curriculum HTML entities are decoded as plain text. Philosophy scraper diagnostics are replaced with understandable provisional-data explanations; unverified figures remain flagged, and original source notes remain in a collapsed section.
